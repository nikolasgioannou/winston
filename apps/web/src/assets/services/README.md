# Service artwork

These locally bundled assets identify existing service connections. Preserve their colors, proportions, and adjacent service labels. They are decorative images inside existing controls, not independent buttons or an indication of endorsement.

- Gmail: [official SVG](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/static/images/icons/product/gmail.svg).
- Google Calendar: [official SVG](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/static/images/icons/product/calendar.svg).
- Telegram: `Logo.svg` from the official archive linked on [Telegram Logos and App Screenshots](https://telegram.org/tour/screenshots). Telegram permits action-button use without implying official representation.

Google's [product compatibility guidelines](https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google/use-cases/product-co-branding/) cover contextual product icons in compatibility lists and action controls. Google's [brand guidelines](https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google/branding-guidelines/how-to-show-googles-brand/) require current artwork and accurate presentation of Google's role.
